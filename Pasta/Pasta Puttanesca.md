
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 1 lb Spaghetti or Linguine
- 3 tbsp Olive oil
- 3–4 cloves Garlic, minced or smashed
- 4–6 Anchovy fillets (or 1 tsp anchovy paste)
- 1 (28 oz) can Whole peeled tomatoes (crushed by hand)
- 1/2 cup Black olives (canned or oil-cured), sliced or halved
- 2 tbsp Capers, drained
- 1 tsp Dried oregano (optional)
- Black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

***DIRECTIONS***:
Prep the Pasta: Boil a large pot of salted water. Add pasta and cook until just shy of al dente.

Melt the Anchovies: In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and anchovies. Stir and press down on the anchovies with a wooden spoon until they "melt" into the oil and the garlic is fragrant (don't let the garlic brown).

Simmer: Add the crushed tomatoes and their juices. Stir in the black olives and capers. Let it simmer for about 10–12 minutes until the sauce thickens and the flavors meld.

Toss: Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the pasta cooking water. Toss for 1 minute so the sauce coats the noodles perfectly.

Finish: Season with a generous amount of black pepper. Garnish with parsley and serve.
