

### ***INGREDIENTS:***

- ​**500g Bife de Frango** (_Thinly sliced chicken breast cutlets_)
- ​**250g Pasta of choice** (_Penne, fettuccine, or rigatoni work brilliantly_)
- ​**1 medium Chalota** (_Shallot, finely diced_)
- ​**3 cloves Alho** (_Garlic, minced_)
- ​**1/2 cup Vinho Verde** (_Dry white wine_)
- ​**3/4 cup Iogurte Grego** (_Plain Greek yogurt, full-fat works best_)
- ​**1 tbsp Pimentão Doce / Paprika** (_Sweet paprika_)
- ​**1 tsp Pimentão Fumado** (_Smoked paprika_)
- ​**1/2 tsp Alho em Pó** (_Garlic powder_)
- ​**1/2 tsp Cebola em Pó** (_Onion powder_)
- ​**1 tbsp Azeite** (_Olive oil_)
- ​**1 tbsp Manteiga** (_Butter_)
- ​**Sal e Pimenta** (_Salt and black pepper to taste_)
- ​**1 tbsp Sumol de Limão** (_Fresh lemon juice, to balance_)

### ***DIRECTIONS***:
Boil the Pasta Water
Prerequisite for sauce emulsification
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Drop your pasta and set a timer for 1 minute less than package directions (it will finish cooking directly in the sauce). Save 1/2 cup of hot pasta water right before draining.

Season & Sear the Chicken
Pat the bifes de frango dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and half of the sweet paprika.
Heat olive oil and butter in a wide skillet over medium-high heat on your stovetop. Add the chicken in a single layer and sear for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove chicken to a plate and cover loosely to keep warm.

Build the Refogado (Aromatic Base)
Turn heat down to medium. In the same skillet (don't wipe out the brown bits!), add the diced shallot and minced garlic. Sauté for about 1 to 2 minutes until translucent and fragrant.
Stir in the remaining sweet paprika and smoked paprika, letting the spices bloom in the fat for 30 seconds.

Deglaze with Vinho Verde
Pour in the Vinho Verde, scraping up all the caramelized chicken juices stuck to the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon. Let the wine simmer rapidly for 2 to 3 minutes until reduced by half and the alcohol smell burns off. Turn heat to low.

Emulsify the Yogurt Sauce
In a small bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt with 2 tablespoons of warm pasta water to temper it (this prevents curdling). Slowly stir the yogurt into the simmering skillet over low heat until smooth and creamy. Squeeze in the lemon juice and taste for salt.
