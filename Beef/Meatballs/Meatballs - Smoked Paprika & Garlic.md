
***INGREDIENTS*** 
Carne picada de bovino (Ground beef): 400g–500g
Ovo (Egg): 1 large
Pão ralado (Breadcrumbs): 1/3 cup
Alho (Garlic): 3 cloves, finely minced
Cebola (Onion): 1/3 cup, finely diced
Queijo (Cheese): 1/4 cup finely grated
Páprika defumada (Smoked paprika): 1 tsp
Pimenta preta e Sal (Black pepper & coarse salt): To taste
Azeite (Olive oil): 2 tbsp for browning
Vinho Verde: 2 tbsp (for pan deglazing)

***DIRECTIONS*** 
Build the Mix: In a mixing bowl, combine the egg, breadcrumbs, minced garlic, finely diced onion, cheese, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Stir until it forms a smooth paste.

Add Beef: Gently fold the carne picada de bovino into the seasoning paste until just combined. Avoid over-kneading so the meatballs remain tender.

Shape: Roll into 12–14 golf-ball-sized meatballs.
Stove-Top Sear: Heat the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs and brown them on all sides for 6–7 minutes until a deep crust forms.

Deglaze: Splash in the Vinho Verde to loosen the savory browned bits (fundo da frigideira) stuck to the pan, rolling the meatballs in the glaze for 1 minute.

Simmer in Sauce: Pour your jar of Molho Bolonhesa straight into the skillet. Reduce heat to low, cover, and let the meatballs simmer gently for 8–10 minutes to finish cooking through and soak up the sauce. Serve over your egg pasta!
