
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 400g - 500g Beef (burger patties crumbled/sliced, or thin strips of bife)
- 1 large Onion (sliced)
- 1 medium Leek (alho francês) (white and light green parts only, sliced and washed thoroughly)
- 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
- 2 tbsp Butter
- 1 tbsp Olive oil
- 200ml Beef broth (caldo de carne)
- 200ml Natas (cooking cream, can sub Greek yogurt)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard (mostarda de Dijon)
- 1 tbsp Soy sauce (molho de soja - optional, for savory depth)
- salt, and black pepper

***DIRECTIONS***:
Season the beef strips generously with salt and pepper.

Heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp of butter in a large pan over high heat. Sear the beef strips quickly until browned on all sides. Remove meat from the pan and set aside.

Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tbsp of butter to the pan. Add the sliced onion and sliced leek. Cook slowly, stirring occasionally, until they are very soft and golden (about 8-10 minutes). Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute.

Pour the beef broth into the pan to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Stir in the mustard and soy sauce. Let it simmer for 3 minutes to reduce slightly.

Turn the heat to low. Add the browned meat (and any juices) back into the pan. Stir in the natas. Let it gently warm through for 2-3 minutes, but do not let it boil rapidly, or the cream might split.

Taste and add more salt/pepper if needed. Serve immediately over white rice (arroz branco) or pasta, garnished with fresh parsley.
