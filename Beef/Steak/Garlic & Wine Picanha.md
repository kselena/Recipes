
***INGREDIENTS:***
- 2 steaks (patted dry with paper towels)
- Salt: Preferably Sal Grosso (coarse), but table salt works
- Garlic: 4–5 cloves, smashed with the flat of a knife (keep the skin on)
- White Wine: Roughly 100ml (half a small glass)
- 1 tbsp Olive Oil and 1 knob of cold butter
- Black Pepper: To taste (add at the end)

***DIRECTIONS:***
Take the meat out of the fridge 15 minutes before cooking. Pat it bone-dry with a paper towel. Season both sides generously with salt.

Get your pan smoking hot with the olive oil. Use tongs to hold the steaks upright on their thin fat-cap edge for about 60 seconds until the fat starts to go translucent and golden.

Lay the steaks flat. Do not touch them for 2 minutes. You want a dark, caramelized crust.

Flip the steaks. Immediately toss in the smashed garlic cloves and the knob of butter.

Once the butter foams, tilt the pan slightly and use a spoon to pour that hot garlic-butter over the steaks for 1 minute.

Pour the white wine directly into the pan (away from the meat if possible). Let it bubble and hiss for 1–2 minutes until the liquid reduces by half.

Move the steaks to a warm plate. Pour the remaining wine-butter-garlic sauce from the pan over them. Cover loosely with foil and wait 5 minutes. This lets the juices reabsorb.
