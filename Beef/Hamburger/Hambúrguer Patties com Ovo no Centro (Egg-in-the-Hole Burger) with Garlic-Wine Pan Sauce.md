
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 2 Pre-made Hamburger Patties
- **2 Eggs** (Small or medium work best so they fit)
- **1 template/glug of Vinho Verde** (White wine for the sauce)
- **2 cloves of Alho** (Garlic), thinly sliced
- **1 tbsp Manteiga** (Butter)
- **1 tbsp Azeite** (Olive oil)
- **Salt and coarse black pepper**

#### ***Directions***

1.Hollow Out the Patties:**2 min. *IF YOU WANT TO; CAN ALWAYS ADD EGG ON TOP*

Using a small cookie cutter, a shot glass, or a knife, cut a clean hole (about 2 inches wide) right out of the center of each pre-made patty. (Save the beef centers! You can fry them up as little chef's snacks next to the burgers).

**2.The Initial Sear:**4 min.

Heat the olive oil in your skillet over medium-high heat on the stove. Add the hollowed-out patties. Sear for 2 minutes until a nice crust forms, then flip them over. Turn the heat down to medium-low immediately.

**3.Drop the Eggs:**5-6 min.

Crack an egg directly into the center hole of each patty. Season the egg with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cover the pan with a lid (or a piece of foil) and let it cook on medium-low for 4 to 5 minutes until the egg white is completely set but the yolk is still beautifully soft and runny. Carefully remove the burgers and place them on your serving plates.

**4.Whip Up the Clean Sauce:**2 min.

Leave the pan on the stove at medium heat. Toss in the sliced garlic and let it sizzle for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the glug of Vinho Verde, scraping up the savory beef bits. Let the wine bubble and reduce by half (about 1 minute), then drop in the butter. Swirl the pan off the heat until the butter melts into a clean, glossy garlic sauce.

> **Chef's Tip:** Covering the pan while the egg cooks is absolutely essential here. On a standard stove, the heat only hits the bottom of the patty. The lid traps the steam, cooking the top of the egg white perfectly while keeping your yolk runny.

### Your Dinner Plate Architecture

Since you want a simple and cohesive meal, let's look at how to balance the plate with the items you mentioned:

- **The Sweet Potato:** Slice your sweet potato into rounds or wedges, toss with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of your paprika, and roast it in your convection oven until caramelized. The natural sweetness pairs beautifully with the sharp, acidic garlic-wine sauce you'll drizzle over the burger.
    
- **The Veggie Choice:** Skip the pasta tonight to keep the plate light. Instead, steam or pan-fry your **frozen broccoli and cauliflower** right in a splash of water, toss them with a little butter, and place them next to the burger.
