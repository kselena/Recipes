
***INGREDIENTS*** 
Bifes de frango: 1–1.5 lbs chicken cutlets (thin cutlets work best here)
Echalotas (Shallots): 2 medium shallots, finely diced
Alho: 2 cloves garlic, minced
Iogurte Grego: ½ cup Greek yogurt (room temperature)
Mostarda: 1 tbsp yellow or Dijon mustard
Vinho Verde: ¼ cup
Temperos: 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ½ tsp paprika, salt, and pepper

***DIRECTIONS***:
Prep & Season: Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika.

Pan-Fry: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook cutlets for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.

Sauté Aromatics: Lower heat to medium. Add diced shallots and minced garlic to the pan, cooking for 2 minutes until soft and fragrant.
Make Sauce: Deglaze with vinho verde and let it bubble down by half (about 1 minute). Turn the heat down to low. In a small bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt and mustard together, then stir into the skillet.

Combine: Return the chicken to the skillet and spoon the sauce over top. Let it warm through for 1–2 minutes on low (do not let it boil, or the yogurt will break).

Chef's Tip: To prevent Greek yogurt from curdling when introduced to heat, bring it to room temperature before cooking, and keep your stove burner on the lowest setting when stirring it into the skillet.
