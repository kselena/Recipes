
***INGREDIENTS***:
- Bifes de Frango: 2-3 thin chicken breast steaks
- Vinho Verde: ½ cup (the crispness of a Loureiro or Alvarinho works best)
- Alcaparras (Capers): 1 tablespoon, drained
- Manteiga sem sal (Unsalted Butter): 2 tablespoons (keep it in the fridge until the last second!)
- Sumo de Limão: Juice of half a lemon
- Pantry Staples: Garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and a neutral oil (like vegetable or sunflower oil).

Side: Your frozen vegetables.

***DIRECTIONS***:
Season the Chicken: Pat the bifes very dry. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and a light dusting of garlic powder.

***The Sear:*** 
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat with 1 tablespoon of oil. Once shimmering, add the chicken. Cook for about 3 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove the chicken to a plate and tent loosely with foil to keep warm.

***Deglaze***: 
Turn the heat down to medium. Pour the Vinho Verde into the hot pan. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up all those delicious brown bits (the sucos) from the bottom. Let the wine bubble and reduce by half (about 3 minutes).

***The Sauce "Emulsion":*** 
Add the alcaparras and the lemon juice. Now, the chef's secret: drop in your cold unsalted butter one tablespoon at a time. Whisk or stir constantly as it melts. The cold butter combined with the acid creates a thick, velvety sauce rather than just melted grease.

***Combine***: 
Slide the chicken (and any juices from the plate) back into the pan. Spoon the sauce over the meat for 30 seconds just to warm through.

***Chef’s Tip:***
Don't over-reduce: When the wine is reducing, you want it to look "syrupy" but still have liquid there. If it goes too far and disappears, just add a tiny splash more wine or water before adding the butter. Since you're using Manteiga sem sal, give the sauce a final taste test—you might want one extra pinch of salt to balance the lemon.
