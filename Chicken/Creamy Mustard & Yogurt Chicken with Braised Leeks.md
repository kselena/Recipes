
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (patted dry)
- 3 large leeks, cleaned and sliced into 1cm rounds
- 200ml chicken stock
- 150g Greek yogurt (full-fat works best to prevent curdling)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- Fresh thyme or parsley
- Olive oil, salt, and plenty of black pepper

***DIRECTIONS:***
Season the chicken generously with salt and pepper.

In a large, cold skillet, place the chicken skin-side down and turn the heat to medium-high.

Let the fat render out until the skin is golden and very crispy (about 8–10 mins).

Flip and sear the bottom for 2 mins, then remove chicken to a plate.

Drain all but about 1 tbsp of the chicken fat from the pan.

Toss in your leeks and a pinch of salt.

Sauté over medium heat for 5 mins until they begin to soften and turn translucent.

Stir in the garlic for the last 30 seconds.

Pour in the chicken stock and stir in the Dijon mustard.

Nestle the chicken back into the pan, skin-side up (keep that skin above the liquid to stay crispy!).

Lower the heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 10–12 mins until the chicken is cooked through.

Remove the pan from the heat entirely.

Take a spoonful of the hot pan liquid and whisk it into your yogurt in a separate small bowl (this "tempers" the yogurt so it doesn't shock and curdle).

Pour the yogurt mixture into the pan and stir gently into the leeks until smooth and creamy.

Garnish with fresh herbs and a final crack of black pepper.

Don't put the pan back on the flame once the yogurt is in.

The residual heat of the chicken and leeks is more than enough to warm the yogurt through without breaking the sauce.
