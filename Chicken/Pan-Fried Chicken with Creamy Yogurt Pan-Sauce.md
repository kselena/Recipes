
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 2 chicken breasts (pounded to even thickness)
- 1 tbsp Butter
- 1 tbsp All-purpose flour
- ½ cup Chicken broth (or water)
- ½ cup Greek yogurt (room temperature is best)
- 1 clove garlic (minced)
- Juice of ¼ lemon
- Fresh parsley or chives
- Salt
- Pepper
- Pinch of paprika

***DIRECTIONS***:
Season your chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and paprika.

Heat a splash of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.

Fry for 4–5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.

Remove the chicken to a plate and tent it loosely with foil to rest.

Turn the heat down to medium-low.

In the same pan (don't wash it!), melt the 1 tbsp of butter.

Once bubbling, whisk in the 1 tbsp of flour.

Stir constantly for about 1 minute until it turns a light golden color and smells slightly nutty.

Slowly pour in the chicken broth while whisking vigorously.

Scrape up all those brown bits from the bottom of the pan—that's where the flavor lives!

Let it simmer for 1 minute until it starts to thicken into a light gravy.

Turn the heat to low.

Stir the yogurt and garlic into the pan.

Whisk until the sauce is completely smooth and creamy.

Note: Do not let the sauce come to a rolling boil now that the yogurt is in there!

Squeeze in the lemon juice and stir in your herbs.

Taste and add more salt or pepper if needed.

Slide the chicken (and any juices from the plate) back into the pan for 30 seconds to coat.
