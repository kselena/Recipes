
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 4–6 thin chicken bifes (cutlets)
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- Salt 
- Black pepper
- 4 cloves garlic (minced)
- ½ cup chicken broth (or dry white wine)
- Juice of 1 large lemon (4Tbs.)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp cold butter

***DIRECTIONS***:
Pat the chicken dry with paper towels.

Season both sides with salt and pepper, then dredge lightly in flour, shaking off the excess.

Heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp of the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

Once the butter foams, add the chicken.

Cook for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown.

Remove the chicken to a plate and cover it with foil.

Lower the heat to medium.

In the same pan, add the minced garlic.

Sauté for just 30–60 seconds until fragrant (don't let it burn!).

Pour in the chicken broth (or wine) and lemon juice.

Use a wooden spoon to scrape up all those delicious brown bits from the bottom of the pan.

Let the liquid simmer and reduce by half (about 3 minutes).

Turn the heat to low and whisk in the remaining 2 tbsp of cold butter until the sauce looks thick and glossy.

Return the chicken (and any juices on the plate) back to the pan for 1 minute to coat.

Top with plenty of fresh parsley.
