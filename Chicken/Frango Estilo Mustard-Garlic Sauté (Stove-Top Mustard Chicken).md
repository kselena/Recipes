
**Ingredients:**

- ​Chicken breast/cutlets (_Peito_ or _Bife de frango_)
- ​1-2 tbsp Portuguese or Dijon mustard (_mostarda_)
- ​1 tsp garlic powder
- ​1/2 tsp cumin (_cominhos_)
- ​1/4 cup _Vinho Verde_ or water
- ​1-2 tbsp cooking fat (Olive oil or butter)
- ​Salt and black pepper

​**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. ​**Prep:** Rub the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and a light pinch of cumin.
2. ​**Whisk the Coating:** In a small bowl, mix your mustard with 1 tablespoon of _vinho verde_ or water to loosen it slightly. Brush half over the chicken.
3. ​**Stove-Top Sear:** Heat your skillet over medium heat with olive oil or butter. Lay the chicken in the pan. Cook for 3–4 minutes until a nice crust forms.
4. ​**Finish:** Turn the chicken over, spoon the remaining mustard mixture on top, and add the rest of the _vinho verde_ to the pan to steam and create a velvety pan glaze. Cook until internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F).
