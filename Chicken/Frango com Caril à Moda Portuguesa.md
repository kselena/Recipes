
## ***INGREDIENTS*** 
Peito de frango (chicken breast) – cut into 1-inch bite-sized cubes
Pó de caril (curry powder) – 1½ tbsp
Polpa de tomate (tomato puree) – 3 tbsp
Alho e Echalotas – 2 garlic cloves minced, 1 shallot minced
Vinho Verde – ¼ cup
Iogurte Grego (Greek yogurt) – 3 tbsp
Páprica Doce (sweet paprika) – ½ tsp
Olive oil, salt, and black pepper

## ***DIRECTIONS***
Bloom the Spices: Heat 1½ tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the minced shallot, garlic, curry powder, and sweet paprika. Cook together for 1 to 2 minutes until the oil turns fragrant and deep yellow.

Brown the Chicken: Turn heat to medium-high. Add the cubed chicken to the pan, stirring to coat every piece in the spice paste. Cook for 4 minutes until the exterior is opaque.

Simmer: Add the polpa de tomate and Vinho Verde. Stir well, cover with a lid, and let it gently simmer for 6–8 minutes until chicken is cooked through.

Enrich: Turn heat off. Stir in the Greek yogurt to form a rich, mild golden sauce. Taste and adjust salt or add a splash of lemon juice if you like extra brightness.

Chef's Tip: Sautéing your curry powder directly in the hot oil (blooming) before adding liquid unlocks fat-soluble aroma compounds that dry powder simply won't release if added later.
