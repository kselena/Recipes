
***INGREDIENTS***:
- Chicken: 2 large chicken breasts (sliced into thin cutlets)
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (full fat works best to prevent curdling)
- 2 tbsp capers (drained)
- juice of half a lemon (2Tbs)
- 2 cloves minced garlic
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Salt 
- Black pepper
- Optional: Fresh parsley for garnish

***DIRECTIONS***:
Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and oregano.

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.

Cook the chicken for about 5–6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.

Remove chicken from the pan and set aside.

Lower the heat to medium.

In the same pan, add the garlic and capers.

Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant (be careful not to burn the garlic).

In a small bowl, whisk the yogurt and lemon juice.

Add a spoonful of the warm pan juices to the yogurt and stir—this prevents the yogurt from breaking when it hits the heat.

Turn the heat to low.

Stir the yogurt mixture into the skillet.

Whisk gently until smooth and heated through (do not let it boil).

Slide the chicken back into the pan, spooning that salty, tangy sauce over the top.

Garnish with parsley.
