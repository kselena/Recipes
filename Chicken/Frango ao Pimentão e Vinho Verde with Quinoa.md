
### INGREDIENTS:

- ​**Chicken:** Cubed _peito de frango_ (chicken breast)
- ​**Base & Aromatics:** 1 shallot (or onion), minced; 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ​**Spices:** 1 tsp _pimentão doce_ (sweet paprika), garlic powder, salt, and black pepper
- ​**Liquids & Acid:** ½ cup _Vinho Verde_, juice of ½ fresh lemon
- ​**Fat:** Olive oil or butter for searing
- ​**Side:** Quinoa vegetable mix

### ​DIRECTIONS:

1. ​**Parch & Sear:** Season the cubed chicken generously with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and _pimentão doce_. Heat olive oil in your skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken cubes in a single layer and sear for 3–4 minutes until golden on the outside. Remove chicken from the pan and set aside.

2. ​**Build the Sauce:** Lower heat to medium. Add minced shallot and garlic, sautéing for 1–2 minutes until soft and fragrant.

3. ​**Deglaze:** Pour in the _Vinho Verde_, scraping up all those caramelized chicken bits (_o fundo da frigideira_) stuck to the pan. Let the wine simmer and reduce by half (about 3 minutes).

4. ​**Finish:** Stir in the lemon juice and a small knob of butter (optional, for gloss). Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Simmer on low for 2–3 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and coated in the glossy glaze.

5. ​**Serve:** Fluff your warmed quinoa vegetable mix and serve the tangy, paprika-laced chicken right on top.

> ​**Chef’s Tip:** To get a rich, restaurant-style glaze on the stovetop without heavy cream, swirl in a tablespoon of cold butter or a dollop of Greek yogurt off the heat right at the very end.
