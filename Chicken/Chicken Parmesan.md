
***INGREDIENTS:***
- 3 Bifes de frango

The Double-Flour Coating: 
- Flour seasoned with garlic powder, Onion powder, paprika,  provincial herbs, chives
- 1 or 2 beaten eggs (start with 1)

The Fry (The Crunch)
- Avocado or olive oil

The "Stove-Top Melt"
- 2 tablespoons of polpa de tomate (tomato sauce)
- Queijo mozarela
- Parmesão

The Steam Trick (Crucial!)
- 1 teaspoon of water

***DIRECTIONS:***
Take your bifes de frango and make sure they are thin. If you use chicken breast, pound them down to about 1 cm thickness.

***Big bowl or plate #1*** Flour seasoned with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika,  provincial herbs, chives

***Bowl #2***: 1 or 2 beaten eggs (start with 1)

The Process: Dip chicken in Flour -> Egg -> Flour again.

Press the second layer of flour in hard with your palms. Let it sit for 2 minutes—this prevents the "crust" from falling off in the pan.

Heat about 1 cm of avocado oil or olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

Fry the chicken for 4–5 minutes per side. You want a deep golden brown.

Remove the chicken to a plate for just a second.

Carefully pour out the excess oil from the pan (leave just a tiny bit).

Turn the heat down to low.

Place the chicken back in the pan.

Spoon 2 tablespoons of polpa de tomate (tomato sauce) onto the center of each piece of chicken.

Pile your queijo mozarela and parmesão on top of the sauce.

Add 1 teaspoon of water to the empty space in the pan (not on the chicken!) and immediately cover the pan with a lid.

The steam trapped inside will melt the cheese in about 60 to 90 seconds without making the bottom of the chicken soggy.
