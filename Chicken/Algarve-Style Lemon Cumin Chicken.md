## INGREDIENTS 
3 - Bife de Frango (Chicken breast cutlets)
Cominhos (Cumin) – 1.5 tsp
Alho (Garlic) – 4 cloves, smashed and minced
Sumo de Limão (Lemon Juice) – juice of 1 lemon (2 - 2-1/2 Tbps)
Mostarda (Dijon or Yellow Mustard) – 1 tsp
Azeite (Olive Oil), Salt, and Pepper
Your vegetable quinoa mix

## DIRECTIONS
In a bowl, whisk together garlic, cumin, lemon juice, mustard, salt, pepper, and a generous splash of olive oil. Coat the chicken and let it sit for 10 to 15 minutes while your skillet heats up or your side dishes cook.

Heat a splash of olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the marinated chicken breasts for 4 to 5 minutes per side until deeply browned and fragrant with citrus and toasted cumin.  Chef’s Tip: Cumin loves high heat, but it can turn bitter if burnt. Make sure your skillet is hot enough to sear the chicken quickly without charring the spice paste black.

Remove the chicken and let it rest for 5 minutes so the juices redistribute.

Slice the chicken against the grain and serve it over a bed of the bright vegetable quinoa, squeezing a little extra fresh lemon on top.
