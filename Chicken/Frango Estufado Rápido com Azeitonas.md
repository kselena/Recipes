
***INGREDIENTS:***
- Peitos de frango (Chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized cubes)
- Tomate aos cubos (Your diced frozen tomatoes, thawed slightly) – about 1 cup
- Polpa de tomate (Tomato purée) – 2 tablespoons to deepen the color and flavor
- Azeitonas pretas (Black olives) – sliced or whole, pitted
- Chalotas (Shallots) & Alho (Garlic) – chopped
- Pau de Canela (Optional cultural twist: a tiny pinch of sweet paprika or a hint of warmth) Can also use fumado.
- Azeite (Olive oil), salt, pepper

***DIRECTIONS:***
- Brown the Meat: Season your chicken pieces with salt, pepper, and sweet paprika. Heat olive oil in your skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken pieces on all sides. They don't need to be fully cooked yet. Remove them from the pan.

- The Refogado: This is the foundation of Portuguese cooking! In the same pan, add your shallots and garlic. Cook slowly until soft and golden.

- Add the Tomatoes: Stir in your diced tomatoes and the polpa de tomate. Let them cook down for about 5 minutes until they begin to break down into a thick sauce. If it gets too thick, add a splash of water or Vinho Verde.

- Braise: Toss in the black olives and return the chicken to the pan. Turn the heat down to low, cover the skillet with a lid, and let it simmer gently for about 10-12 minutes so the chicken absorbs all the rich tomato and olive flavors.

- Chef’s Tip: To elevate your refogado (the garlic and onion base), don't rush it. Letting the shallots get deeply translucent and slightly sweet before adding the tomatoes changes the whole flavor profile of the dish, cutting through the acidity of the frozen tomatoes.
