
***INGREDIENTS***:
3 chicken steaks

***The Coating***
3 tbsp flour
pinch of salt
black pepper

***The Sear***
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter

***The "Portugal-Style" Sauce***
2 cloves of minced garlic
100ml of dry white wine (like a Vinho Verde)
2 tbsp lemon juice
1.5 tbsp of capers (alcaparras)

***The Finish***
1 tbsp of cold butter

***DIRECTIONS***:
Take your 3 chicken steaks. If one is significantly thicker than the others, give it a quick pound with a heavy skillet so they all cook at the same speed.

On a plate, mix 3 tbsp flour, a pinch of salt, and a good crack of black pepper. Dredge each steak until lightly coated.

In a wide frying pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter over medium-high heat.

Once the butter is bubbling, lay in your 3 steaks.

Cook for 3 minutes on the first side until golden, flip, and cook for 2–3 minutes on the other.

Remove the steaks to a plate.

Lower the heat to medium. In the same pan, add 2 cloves of minced garlic. Stir for 30 seconds.

Pour in 100ml of dry white wine (like a Vinho Verde) and 2 tbsp lemon juice.

Use a spatula to scrape the bottom of the pan to release the flavor. Let it bubble and reduce for about 3 minutes.

Stir in 1.5 tbsp of capers (alcaparras).

Turn the heat to low.

Add 1 tbsp of cold butter. Whisk or stir it quickly until it melts into a glossy sauce.

Place the 3 steaks back in the pan for 1 minute, flipping them once to coat them thoroughly.
