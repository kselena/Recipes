
***INGREDIENTS:***
- Bifes de frango (Chicken cutlets) or Peitos de frango (Chicken breasts, sliced thin)
- Alcaparras (Capers) – about 1-2 tablespoons
- Chalotas (Shallots) & Alho (Garlic) – finely chopped
- Vinho Verde – about 1/4 cup for deglazing
- Mostarda (Dijon or Whole Grain mustard) – 1 tablespoon
- Iogurte Grego (Greek yogurt) – 2 tablespoons (used at the very end to create a creamy texture without heavy cream)
- Azeite (Olive oil), salt, black pepper, and a pinch of Alho em pó (garlic powder)

***DIRECTIONS:***
- Sear the Chicken: Season your chicken cutlets with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat a splash of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken until golden brown on both sides (about 3-4 minutes per side). Remove the chicken from the pan and set it aside on a plate (it will finish cooking in the sauce).

- Build the Base: Lower the heat to medium. In the same pan, add your chopped shallots and garlic. Sauté for 2-3 minutes until translucent and fragrant, scraping up the browned bits from the chicken.

- Deglaze: Pour in the Vinho Verde, letting it bubble rapidly for a minute or two until the smell of alcohol dissipates and the liquid reduces by half.

- Create the Sauce: Stir in the mustard and the capers. Lower the heat to a simmer. Stir in the Greek yogurt quickly to incorporate it smoothly into the warm liquid.

- Simmer and Finish: Return the chicken and any resting juices back to the skillet. Let everything simmer gently for 3-5 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce coats the back of a spoon. Finish with a small squeeze of fresh lemon juice.

- Chef’s Tip: When adding Greek yogurt to a warm skillet sauce, always lower the heat right down first. High heat can cause the yogurt to split or curdle. Stirring it in briskly at a gentle simmer ensures a smooth, glossy emulsion.
