
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 1/2 cup vinho verde 
- 1/2 cup Chicken Broth: Low sodium is best so you can control the salt.
- 2 cloves Garlic: Minced finely.
- 1 Small Shallot: Finely diced (adds a subtle sweetness).
- 2 tbsp Cold Butter: Cut into small cubes.
- 1 tbsp Lemon Juice: Freshly squeezed.
- 1 tsp Flour: Optional, for a thicker "gravy" style.
- Fresh Herbs: Parsley or thyme work beautifully.

***DIRECTIONS***:
Sauté the Aromatics: After cooking your chicken, remove it from the pan. Leave about 1 tablespoon of the chicken fat (or add a splash of olive oil). Sauté the shallots and garlic for about 1 minute until fragrant.

Deglaze: Pour in the white wine. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up the brown bits (the fond) from the bottom of the pan—that's where the flavor lives!

Reduce: Add the chicken broth and lemon juice. Bring to a simmer and let it bubble away until the liquid reduces by about half.

Emulsify: Turn the heat to low. Stir in the cold butter cubes one at a time, whisking constantly. This creates that velvety, glossy texture.

Finish: Stir in your fresh herbs and season with salt and pepper to taste. Pour immediately over your chicken.

Quick Tips for Success: The "Cold Butter" Rule: Make sure your butter is cold when it hits the pan. If the sauce is too hot or the butter is warm, the sauce might "break" (separate into oil).

Quick Tips for Success: Substitution: If you want a creamier sauce (like a Chardonnay sauce), stir in 1/4 cup of heavy cream right before you add the butter.

Quick Tips for Success: No Wine? You can substitute extra chicken broth with a teaspoon of white wine vinegar, though you’ll miss that specific depth of flavor.
