***INGREDIENTS***:
- 2 cups Coconut water (The gold standard for natural potassium)
- 1 cup Plain water
- 1/4 cup Tart cherry juice or Pomegranate juice (High in antioxidants and magnesium)
- 1 tbsp Fresh lime juice
- 1 pinch of Salt (Optional: Just ~1/16 tsp. You need a tiny bit for the glucose to transport, but it's negligible compared to commercial drinks.)

***DIRECTIONS***:
Mix: Combine all liquids in a large pitcher.
Stir: If you chose to add that tiny pinch of salt, stir until completely dissolved.
Serve: Best served very cold.

***High-Potassium Add-ins***
If you want to boost the electrolyte profile without touching the salt shaker, try incorporating these:
- Cream of Tartar: A secret weapon in the "Adrenal Cocktail" world. Adding just 1/8 tsp provides a massive boost of potassium (about 250mg) without any sodium.
- Magnesium Drops: You can add flavorless ionic magnesium liquid to any drink to help with muscle cramps.
- Watermelon Juice: If you have a blender, watermelon is roughly 92% water and contains citrulline, which helps with blood flow.
