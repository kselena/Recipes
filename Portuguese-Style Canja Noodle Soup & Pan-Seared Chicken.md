
## Ingredients 
1 large Bife de frango (Chicken breast)
1 pack Ramen noodles (discard flavor packet)
1 cup Riced vegetables
2 tbsp Vinho Verde (Portuguese green wine)
2 cloves Alho (Garlic), minced
1 Eschalota (Shallot), diced
1/2 tsp Pimentão doce (Sweet paprika)
1 tbsp Lemon juice & 1 tbsp Olive oil
Salt, black pepper, and water/broth

## DIRECTIONS

Season Chicken: Slice bife de frango into cutlets. Rub with minced garlic, pimentão doce, salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice.

Sear on Stove: Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove and set aside to rest.

Build the Broth: In the same skillet, saute diced shallot until soft. Deglaze the pan with Vinho Verde, scraping up the browned bits. Add 2 cups of water (or broth) and bring to a simmer.

Cook Noodles & Veggies: Add the ramen noodles and riced vegetables directly into the simmering broth. Cook on the stovetop for 3 minutes until noodles are tender.

Serve: Ladle the broth, riced veg, and ramen into a bowl, then top with sliced seared chicken breast.
