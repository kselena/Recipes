
***INGREDIENTS:***
- 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar
- 8 slices crisp bacon crumbled
- 1/4 cup sliced green onions (finely sliced)
- 1/2 cup mayo

***DIRECTIONS:***
Mix together, spread on, party ryes, broil until bubbly, doesn't take long!

Note: I make a double or triple batch + keep in the fridge for about 1-2 weeks.
