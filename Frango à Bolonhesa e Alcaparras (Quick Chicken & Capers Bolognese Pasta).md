

## ***INGREDIENTS*** 
Peito de frango (Chicken breast): 400g, cut into small 2cm cubes
Massa (Penne, Rigatoni, or Spaghetti): 250g
Molho à Bolonhesa (Jarred Bolognese sauce): 1 cup (approx. 250g)
Alcaparras (Capers): 2 tbsp, rinsed
Azeitonas pretas (Pitted black olives): 1/3 cup, sliced
Vinho Verde: 1/3 cup
Alho (Garlic): 3 cloves, minced
Echalota (Shallot) or Cebola (Onion): 1 small, finely diced
Azeite (Olive oil): 2 tbsp
Seasonings: Pimentão fumado (Smoked paprika), garlic powder, black pepper

## ***DIRECTIONS***
Prep the Pasta: Bring a pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil on your stove. Add the pasta and cook until 2 minutes short of package instructions (al dente). Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta cooking water, then drain (I  GOD IT ASIDE BUT NEVER USED IT)

Sear the Chicken: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a deep skillet over medium-high heat on the stove. Season the cut peito de frango with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Sear for 4–5 minutes until nicely golden brown on the outside. Transfer the chicken to a side plate.
Build the Sauce: Lower heat to medium. Add 1 tbsp olive oil, the diced shallots, and garlic to the same pan. Sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Deglaze the pan with vinho verde, using a wooden spoon to scrape up all the delicious browned bits stuck to the bottom. Let the wine reduce by half (about 2 minutes).
Simmer: Stir in the jarred molho à bolonhesa, polpa de tomate, rinsed capers, and black olives. Bring to a low simmer for 5 minutes to let the flavors meld.
Combine & Serve: Return the seared chicken breast cubes and cooked pasta to the skillet. Pour in a splash of reserved pasta water. Toss everything together over low heat for 2 minutes until the sauce coats every piece of pasta and the chicken is fully cooked through.
Chef's Tip: Store-bought molho à bolonhesa can sometimes lean slightly sweet or heavy on sodium. Adding a splash of dry vinho verde and acid-rich polpa de tomate directly to the pan cuts that commercial sweetness, making the sauce taste homemade and vibrant!
