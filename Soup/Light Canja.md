
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 2 large chicken breasts (approx. 400g-500g)
- 2 large carrots (diced)
- 1 leek (sliced)
- 1 medium onion
- 2 cloves of garlic (minced)
- 1 bay leaf (louro)
- A big bunch of fresh mint (hortelã)
- 1.5 liters of water (or low-sodium chicken stock)
- Salt
- Black pepper
- A squeeze of lemon juice

***DIRECTIONS***:
Place the chicken breasts, onion (cut in half), bay leaf, and garlic in a large pot with the water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook for about 15–20 minutes until the chicken is opaque throughout.

Remove the chicken and set it aside to cool slightly. Discard the onion halves and the bay leaf. Use two forks to shred the chicken into fine strips.

Add the diced carrots and sliced leeks to the bubbling broth. Simmer for 10 minutes or until the carrots are tender.

Tip: Adding more carrots or even some diced celery increases the volume of the soup without adding many calories.

Add the shredded chicken back into the pot. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Just before serving, stir in a handful of chopped fresh mint and a squeeze of lemon juice. The acidity makes the soup feel "lighter" and more satisfying.
