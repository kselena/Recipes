
***Ingredients:***
 * **6 cups** high-quality chicken broth
 * **1 lb** cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie chicken works perfectly)
 * **1/2 cup** uncooked Arborio or long-grain white rice (or orzo)
 * **2 large** eggs (at room temperature)
 * **1/2 cup** fresh lemon juice (about 2 large lemons)
 * Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste
 * Fresh dill for serving (optional but highly recommended)

***Directions:***
 1. Simmer the rice - 15-20 min
   Bring the chicken broth to a boil in a large pot. Stir in the rice, reduce the heat to a simmer, and cover. Let it cook until the rice is tender (about 15 to 20 minutes). Stir in your shredded chicken during the last 5 minutes just to warm it through, then turn the heat off completely.
   
 2. Whip the egg-lemon base - 2 min
   While the pot cools slightly, whisk the eggs in a medium bowl until they are completely smooth and frothy. Slowly pour in the lemon juice while continuing to whisk until fully combined.
   
 3. Temper the eggs - 3 min
   This is the crucial step! Ladle out about 2 cups of the hot broth from your pot into a measuring cup. While whisking the egg-lemon mixture constantly with one hand, slowly drizzle the hot broth into the bowl with your other hand. This raises the temperature of the eggs gently so they won't curdle.
   
 4. Thicken the soup - 2 min
   Slowly pour the warmed egg-lemon-broth mixture back into the main pot, stirring the soup continuously.
   
 5. Finish and serve - 1 min
   Turn the heat back on to the absolute lowest setting for just 1 to 2 minutes to let the soup thicken slightly—do not let it boil, or the eggs will separate. Season generously with salt and pepper. Ladle into bowls and top with fresh dill and a slice of lemon.
   
> **Reheating Tip:** Because of the delicate egg emulsion, try not to microwave leftover Avgolemono on high heat. Reheat it gently on the stove over low heat, stirring frequently, to keep that perfectly velvety texture.
