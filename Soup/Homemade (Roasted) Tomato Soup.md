
***Ingredients***:
- 2 kg Roma/plum tomatoes, halved
- 500 g–1 kg cherry tomatoes
- 2 onions, cut into wedges
- 2 whole garlic bulbs (Jessica used 7)
- 1–2 cans whole peeled tomatoes
- 2–3 tbsp tomato paste
- 4–5 cups vegetable broth
- Olive oil
- Salt + pepper
- Pinch chili flakes
- Optional smoked paprika
- Optional Fresh basil
- Optional splash cream
- Tiny splash balsamic vinegar

***Directions***:

Roast vegetables
Preheat oven to 220°C.

Spread tomatoes and onions on sheet pans. Drizzle generously with olive oil, salt, and pepper.

Leave cherry tomatoes whole.

For garlic:
- cut tops off bulbs
- drizzle each with 1–2 tsp olive oil
- wrap completely in foil

Roast everything 35–50 minutes until:
- tomatoes are collapsed/caramelized
- cherry tomatoes are blistered
- onions are soft
- garlic is very soft

2. Build soup
In a large pot:
- cook tomato paste in a little olive oil for 1–2 min

Add:
- roasted vegetables
- squeezed roasted garlic
- canned tomatoes
- broth
- optional smoked paprika

Simmer 15–20 minutes.

3. Blend
Add optimal basil and blend until smooth.

Adjust with more broth if needed.

If acidic:
- add tiny pinch sugar

4. Finish
Add:
- tiny splash balsamic
- optional cream

Serve with grilled cheese.
