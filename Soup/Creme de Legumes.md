
***INGREDIENTS***:
- 3 large potatoes
- 2 medium carrots
- 1 large onion
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 large zucchini (peeled) or a small head of cauliflower
- A generous glug of high-quality extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt

***DIRECTIONS***:
Prep the Veggies: Peel everything. Chop them into rough, even cubes. Since you're blending it all anyway, don't worry about knife skills—just keep the sizes similar so they cook at the same rate.

Boil: Place the vegetables in a large pot. Add just enough water to barely cover them. If you add too much water now, the soup will be thin and runny. You can always add more water later to reach your preferred thickness.

Simmer: Bring to a boil, then drop to a simmer. Cook for about 20–25 minutes until the potatoes and carrots are fork-tender.

The Smooth Transformation: Use an immersion blender (hand blender) directly in the pot. Blend until there is absolutely no trace of a chunk.

Tip: If you want it "restaurant-level" smooth, pass the blended liquid through a fine-mesh strainer (chinois) to catch any remaining fibers.

The "Crucial" Finish: Once the soup is smooth, stir in a healthy splash of extra virgin olive oil and season with salt. Let it simmer for another 2–3 minutes. This emulsifies the oil into the soup, giving it a glossy finish and a richer mouthfeel.
